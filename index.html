How to read the sources

Each agency keeps its own register of seizures: the army (SEDENA), the navy (SEMAR), the Federal Police and then the Guardia Nacional, the federal prosecutors (FGR), and state police and prosecutors. The same register reaches us in several versions: an answer to a transparency request, a later pull of the same system, a yearly public sheet, or a table in the annex of the president's yearly report. The versions can differ because the system is revised over time.

Main source: the version drawn in the one-line-per-agency view, the longest yearly series of that agency's own records. Comparison only: pooled totals of all federal agencies, or compiled files that mix several registers. These are never added to agency lines, since that would count the same seizure twice.

Kilograms, pills and ampoules are separate series and are never added. Tons, grams and milligrams were converted to kilograms. Cannabis plants are kept apart from marijuana.

Download the data: dash_seizures.csv (one row per source, agency, place, year, drug and unit) and dash_sources.csv (one row per source, with its years, detail and notes).

Known gaps: the navy and Guardia Nacional answers stop in 2022 and 2021; after that the Guardia Nacional appears only in INEGI's police census. The INEGI prosecutor census has no table for 2020 and 2021. SEDENA's yearly public sheets count marijuana seized plus incinerated, so they run well above the register. The Informe annex and GI-TOC tables cover January to June of 2024 only.
