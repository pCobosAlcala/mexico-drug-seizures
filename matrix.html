Ways to extend the seizures row

Sources that exist but we do not hold yet, mostly for the years after 2022.

  • SEDENA's full file behind the GI-TOC tables (transparency folio 330026424002125), to June 2024, probably one row per seizure. GI-TOC published tables built from it; the file itself would come from the transparency platform or from GI-TOC.
  • Navy and Guardia Nacional seizures after 2022. The answers we hold stop in 2022 and 2021. Nothing newer is published; it needs new transparency requests.
  • Gabinete de Seguridad daily bulletins, from November 2024: one entry per operation, with what was seized and how many people were detained. Free text; it needs a scraper.
  • SEDENA and FGR fentanyl by state under López Obrador. Reported in the press (N+, Vanguardia) without a published file; a transparency request should get it.
  • Informe de Seguridad deck, April 2023: national fentanyl by month in kilograms, pills and ampoules. A public PDF on gob.mx, not yet downloaded.
  • Calderón's Informe annexes with state tables, 2007 to 2012. Listed in the annex index but not found in the archives.
  • FGR CENAPI answer (folio 0001700006421), 2000 to 2020 by state, all agencies pooled. We hold it, but it is a scanned PDF that needs text recognition.
